RULES OF THUMB
- Use SVG wherever possible. Never retype "relate"; use the supplied artwork.
- Clear space = width of one arch leg on every side. Min size: logo 120 px, icon 16 px.
- White logo on navy or dark; navy logo on white or light. No recoloring, stretching, outlines or shadows.
- Archie supports the logo, never replaces it. One flair version per layout.
- Typography: Public Sans (headings SemiBold, body Regular), IBM Plex Mono for code.
